sidekick v0.2.1 brings every validated embedding model to grade A on the Neural Engine: worst-case cosine of 0.9999 or better against the published fp32 model, over the parity suite's 51 adversarial inputs. The shipped binaries are unchanged apart from their version; the gains come from the conversion tools, so re-convert to get them.

## Upgrading

- **Re-convert LFM2.5-Embedding-350M, F2LLM-v2-160M and gte-modernbert-base** with this release's converters in `tools/`. Artifacts converted earlier keep working, less accurately. If you converted EmbeddingGemma before v0.2.0, re-convert it too.
- **LFM2.5 gets 13–16% slower on the ANE**, from its explicit SiLU. The other re-conversions cost nothing measurable.

## What the converters fix

Three limits of ANE arithmetic, found while diagnosing these models, are now conversion rules in `docs/MODELS.md`:

- **The ANE's `linear` op loses precision on small inputs.** Its relative error is about 3e-4 divided by the input's rms. The converters scale small inputs up by calibrated powers of two, cancelled exactly downstream. This was LFM2.5's main loss and part of F2LLM's.
- **Core ML's built-in GELU and SiLU are coarse on the ANE.** The converters build them from tanh, and fail if a built-in one survives conversion. coremltools fuses `x·sigmoid(x)` back into SiLU, so that form doesn't avoid it. This was LFM2.5's other loss and F2LLM's main one.
- **The ANE's `linear` op saturates above 32,768**, half of fp16's range. gte-modernbert's layer-15 MLP produced 35k–50k on delimiter tokens, so the old artifact ran on -inf inside the graph and was rescued only by downstream saturation. The converter now rescales the residual stream by a power of two chosen automatically, keeping every calibrated linear output under 85% of the limit, and reports the remaining headroom.

The LFM2.5, F2LLM and gte-modernbert converters also check more strictly, as EmbeddingGemma's has since v0.2.0. Their long parity texts actually run: LFM2.5's, like EmbeddingGemma's, had been silently skipped for exceeding its bucket. They check that pad content can't change the output, treat NaN as a failure, and gate the ANE path at 0.999.

The F2LLM converter works again. With current torch and coremltools, its `repeat_kv` rewrite had crashed conversion.

## Parity suite

- The ANE bucket-invariance gate is now 0.9999, up from 0.995. The old bound only accommodated the precision losses above; every model now measures 0.99995 or better.
- The M1 Max accuracy floors are re-measured for the three re-converted models.

## Release process

Release notes now live in `docs/releases/`. They're published as the release's description on the Releases page and ship in the archive as `RELEASE_NOTES.md`. The release workflow refuses a tag whose notes are missing or whose version doesn't match the workspace.

## Known issues

- **Core ML's compile caches grow quickly.** Core ML caches compiled models per executable in `~/Library/Caches/<executable>/com.apple.e5rt.e5bundlecache`. Converting models from Python, or running `ane_check` or the parity suite, can grow these caches to tens of GB, sometimes over 100 GB in a day of conversions. They are safe to delete.
