Grade

A to F, the parity suite's letter. F is a failed hard gate. Compare letters within a task.

Nines

−log₁₀ of the worst error: 1 − cosine for embedders (4 nines is a cosine of 0.9999), the largest probability change Δp for classifiers and rerankers (3 nines is a Δp of 0.001). Higher is better. The grade, not the nines, is comparable across tasks' scales.

ms / input

Median per input from the grading run, one input at a time. Dotted values come from a machine that wasn't idle, or whose load wasn't recorded. Fixed-length benchmark timings, where they exist, are in the value's tooltip.

Memory

The process's own footprint once loaded, where benchmarked; hover or tap a value for how it was measured. On Apple silicon a process's count misses Neural Engine memory held by the system, so those rows also give the whole machine's change where it stood clear of noise.

Compare scores within one benchmark only. † marks tasks in the model's training data. Hover or tap a score for its source.

Reading a score

A benchmark may have run on longer inputs than a build takes: each score's tooltip gives the run's context, and each row its build's max tokens. MTEB prompts many models per task, and sidekick always adds its own prefix: for EmbeddingGemma and (outside retrieval and reranking) e5-small-v2 a client can't reproduce the run's prompts, and should expect lower classification, clustering and STS. A grade-A path reproduces the model closely enough for its score to carry over (embedders: worst cosine ≥ 0.9999; classifiers: worst Δp ≤ 0.001 or within 1.25× the fp16 ceiling); C, D or int8 may not. A few hundred labelled examples from your own data decide. More in docs/MODELS.md.
